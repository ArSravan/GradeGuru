GradeGuru — Student Risk Predictor

Enter student attributes from the UCI Student Performance dataset. The app predicts final grade (G3) and assigns a risk band. Use Early for early-warning; use Full when you also know G1 and G2.

Flow: /dashboard → /result

Input Form

Pick a mode, fill the fields, then click Predict. (This submits to /result and shows the prediction on a new page.)

Tip: Use dataset formats: Sex (M/F), Address (U/R), and yes/no fields.
If you get “Missing required features”, check /api/schema.
